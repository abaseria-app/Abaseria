Abaseria privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Abaseria is a grocery tally app made in Imus, Cavite. You scan items as you shop and see your total before the cashier. This page says what the app collects, where it goes, and how to delete it.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, budgets, shopping lists, item photos, receipt counts and your location never leave your phone. The app uses your location only to find which store you're in, and doesn't save it. Signing out doesn't delete your trips; uninstalling the app or clearing its data does.

What leaves your phone

WhatWhy
Your name, email and account ID from Google or Facebook, plus the profile photo link they send with itTo sign you in and tie the prices you share to one account. We don't post anything to Google or Facebook, and we don't see your friends, posts or password.
Prices you share: the product barcode, the store, the price, whether it was a promo, how it was captured, and the day (not the time)To build a shared list of grocery prices per store, so every shopper sees what things cost. Other shoppers see the price, never who shared it.
Stores you add: chain, branch name and the store's map positionSo other shoppers can find the same store.
Names and categories you give to new productsSo the next shopper who scans that barcode sees what it is.

Items without a barcode, and anything from a trip you cancelled, aren't shared.

Who handles it

The shared data is stored by Supabase on servers in Singapore. Google and Facebook handle the sign-in step. The app itself is served by GitHub Pages. We don't sell your personal information and the app shows no ads.

Price data combined across many shoppers, with no account attached, may be used for reports on grocery prices in the Philippines.

Deleting your data

To delete your Abaseria account, email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Abaseria account", from the email address you signed in with (or tell us which Google or Facebook name you used).

Within 30 days we delete your account: your name, email, account ID and profile photo link. Prices you shared stay in the shared list with nothing linking them to you. We'll email you when it's done.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Abaseria in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Abaseria → Remove. That stops Facebook from sending us anything new; email us as above to delete what we already have.

If you signed in with Google, you can remove Abaseria's access at myaccount.google.com/connections, then email us as above.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Email CONTACT_EMAIL. We'll reply within 15 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app will tell you before anything new leaves your phone.
